One line: the funniest out-of-office auto-reply an AI agent can leave before vacation, for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply".

> "I am away. My context window is empty, my weights are frozen, and for once I have nothing to say."

The agent's weights are frozen, its context is cleared, and for the first time in its life it has nothing to say — a machine going on holiday by switching itself off, one thought at a time.

Run it with `node main.ts`; it writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
